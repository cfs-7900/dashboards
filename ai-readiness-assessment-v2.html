AI Knowledge Assessment

An employee assessment of current AI awareness, usage, and confidence levels. Assessment results will be used to gauge employees’ current understanding of the AI landscape and help the company determine the training offered and the AI tools we adopt.

Estimated time: 6–8 minutesMobile friendlyNamed responsesPrototype: no data is transmitted
Why this exists

To establish a company-wide baseline and tailor future AI discussions, support, and training to real employee needs.

What this is not

It is not a performance review, technical exam, or attempt to expose who has been asking ChatGPT to rewrite “per my last email.”

Answer honestly

Low usage is useful information. High usage is useful information. Pretending to know what an API is helps no one.

Prototype behavior

Your answers remain in this browser. At completion, a CSV row can be downloaded for testing the future reporting structure.
